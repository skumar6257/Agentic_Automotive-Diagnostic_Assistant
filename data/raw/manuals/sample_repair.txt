# Spark Plug Replacement Procedure
To fix a P0300 code, you may need to replace the spark plugs. 
Step 1: Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
Step 2: Remove the engine cover.
Step 3: Disconnect the ignition coil connectors.
Step 4: Remove the ignition coils.
Step 5: Use a 5/8 inch spark plug socket to remove the old spark plugs. 
Torque Specification: Tighten new spark plugs to 18 ft-lbs.
